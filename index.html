Search inside a club or team

Use this when the username looks nothing like the name. Many players join their university's club on Chess.com or team on Lichess, and their profile name gives them away. It uses the name, country and rating above.

How it works

  1. Enter the opponent's name. Add a country or rating range if you know them.
  2. Pick the account that looks right on Chess.com, Lichess or both.
  3. Scout builds the report: their openings, a prep sheet, and a practice drill against their real moves.

Tournament players

Open the tournament on chess-results.com, select the starting rank list or the pairings, copy the table and paste it here. This page never contacts Chess-Results; it only reads what you paste.
