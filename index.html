Chess Scout

Find your opponent's accounts

Enter what you know. The tool checks hundreds of likely usernames and public profiles on Chess.com and Lichess, tries every username it finds on the other site too, and ranks the matches. You choose the right one.

Search inside a club or team

Use this when the username looks nothing like the name. Many players join their university's club on Chess.com or team on Lichess, and their profile name gives them away. It uses the name, country and rating from the form above.
